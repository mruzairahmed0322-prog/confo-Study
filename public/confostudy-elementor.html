Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about admissions, classes, fees, and learning at ConfoStudy.

1. How can I take admission?
You can apply online or contact us.
2. Which boards do you teach?
We teach students from all major Pakistani boards, including Federal Board (FBISE), Sindh Board, Punjab Board, KPK Board, Aga Khan University Examination Board (AKU-EB), Cambridge O/A Levels, and Cambridge IGCSE.
3. Are classes live or recorded?
Most classes are live, and recordings may be provided when available.
4. What documents are required?
Father’s CNIC (Front & Back), Previous Mark Sheet, and Student Photograph. Student B-Form is also requested if available.
5. Is there any discount?
Yes, students receive a discount on one-time payment. For Class IX & X, a Rs.2,000 discount is available (installments Rs.4,000 × 3). For Class XI & XII, a Rs.3,000 discount is available (installments Rs.5,000 × 3).
6. How are exams conducted?
Online weekly, monthly tests, mid-terms, and pre-board examinations are conducted according to the student’s board.
7. What are the passing marks?
40% of the total marks.
8. Can students study from any city?
Yes. Students from anywhere in the world can join our online classes.
9. Do you provide one-on-one support?
Yes, students receive personalized academic guidance.
10. What are the class timings?
Different morning and evening batches are available depending on the course.
11. What can we get from ConfoStudy?
We will provide updated notes, MCQ's with solutions, PYQ's, doubt classes, and teacher support.
